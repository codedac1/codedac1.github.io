How to keep a stopwatch on screen on a Windows PC

Updated 2026-10-30

Timing a speedrun, a workout, a task or a meeting is easier when the stopwatch stays in view instead of hiding behind the window you are using. Windows has one built-in way, and a floating-timer app adds more.

Option 1: the Clock app's Stopwatch with "Keep on top"

The Clock app that comes with Windows has a Stopwatch tab. Start the stopwatch and choose Keep on top, and it shows in a small window that stays above other windows.

It is quick and needs nothing extra. It shows one stopwatch in a fixed small window.

Option 2: stopwatches that float as cards

FloatTimer+ for Windows (our app) floats stopwatches, timers and counters above every window as small pastel cards, each timed separately. A card never takes focus, so clicking it leaves your cursor where you were typing or playing.

  1. Install FloatTimer+ from the Microsoft Store; it lives in the system tray.
  2. Create a stopwatch and float it.
  3. Start, pause and restart with the ▶/⏸/↻ button on the card itself — no need to open the app.
  4. Drag the card anywhere or resize it, and collapse it into a small bubble or push it against the screen edge when you want the space back.

If a game covers the stopwatch

No window can stay on top of a game running in exclusive full-screen mode. Switch the game's display mode to borderless or windowed in its settings, and floating windows — the Clock app's or FloatTimer+ cards — show above it again.

Free and Pro

You can make as many stopwatches, timers and counters as you like, with no ads. The free version floats two cards at a time; Pro is a one-time purchase that floats as many as you want.

Questions

Does Windows have a stopwatch that stays on top?

Yes. The Clock app's Stopwatch has Keep on top, which shows one stopwatch in a small window above others. For several stopwatches, timers or counters at once, use a floating-timer app.

Why does my stopwatch disappear when I start a game?

The game is probably in exclusive full-screen mode, which covers every other window. Switch it to borderless or windowed mode in the game's display settings.

Can I run more than one stopwatch at a time?

With FloatTimer+ you can make as many as you like, each timed separately. The free version shows two floating cards at once; Pro floats more.

How to keep a timer on screen while using other apps on Android

Updated 2026-10-14

The timer in the Clock app runs in the background, but you can't see it while you are in another app — you have to pull down notifications or switch back. For studying with a lecture, timing a presentation, or a game, it helps to have the time on screen the whole time.

Set it up with FloatTimer+

FloatTimer+ (our app) floats timers, stopwatches and tally counters over any app as small cards you can drag anywhere.

  1. Install FloatTimer+ from Google Play and allow "Display over other apps".
  2. Create a timer — any hours, minutes and seconds, or a quick preset such as 25 minutes.
  3. Float it and open the app you are using. Start, pause and reset from the card itself.
  4. Fold it into a bubble when you need the space; the time keeps running.

More than one at a time

You can make as many timers, counters and stopwatches as you like for free, and they keep running. The free version shows one on screen at a time; Pro floats as many as you want at once, or a short ad unlocks that for 24 hours.

Questions

Can I see a timer over YouTube or a game?

Yes. A floating timer stays on top of any app, including videos and games, once you allow "Display over other apps".

Is there a Pomodoro preset?

FloatTimer+ has 1, 5, 10, 25, 30 and 60-minute quick presets, so a 25-minute focus timer is one tap.

How to see crypto prices while using other apps on Android

Updated 2026-10-14

Exchange apps show prices inside the app or in a home-screen widget, so checking the market means leaving whatever you are doing. A floating ticker keeps the prices you follow on screen over any app.

Set up a floating ticker with FloatCrypto+

FloatCrypto+ (our app) floats a price ticker over every app — YouTube, news, a game, a chat. Drag it anywhere, resize it and lower its opacity so it doesn't cover what you are reading.

  1. Install FloatCrypto+ from Google Play and allow "Display over other apps".
  2. Add the coins you follow to your watchlist and drag them into your order.
  3. Turn on the floating ticker and place it where you like.
  4. Set an upper or lower price alert from a coin's ⋮ menu to be notified when the market crosses it.

Good to know

Prices come from Binance with an automatic fallback source and may be delayed by up to 30 seconds (10 seconds on Pro). They are for reference only, and the app does not trade or connect to an exchange account. Nothing here is investment advice.

Free follows 2 coins with 1 alert. A Free Pass opens more coins or longer charts for 24 hours with a short ad, and Pro gives 10 coins, unlimited alerts, a 10-second refresh and no ads.

Overview

FloatCalc+ is a floating calculator that stays on top of any app, so you never have to close your shopping, banking or browser screen just to work something out. The calculator, unit converter and currency converter share one draggable window and one familiar keypad, and the result appears as you type.

Select a number in any app and tap “Calculate with FloatCalc+” to bring it straight in, or open the panel from the Quick Settings tile, a home screen widget or the notification. Shrink it to an icon at the edge of the screen when you need the space. Exchange rates come from the European Central Bank and refresh daily, cached so everything keeps working offline.

FAQ

Do I have to close my app every time I want to calculate?

No. FloatCalc+ is a floating window that stays on top of other apps, so you can work out a discount or convert a price without leaving your shopping, banking or browser screen. Select a number in the app and tap “Calculate with FloatCalc+”, or just type it in, get your answer, and carry on right where you were.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

From the European Central Bank, refreshed once a day. The line under the amounts shows the rate and its date, and Settings updates the rates on demand. You can convert between any pair of 30+ currencies. The daily cache keeps it working offline.

Does it work offline?

The calculator and unit converter always work offline. Currency conversion uses the last rates you downloaded.

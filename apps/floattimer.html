Overview

FloatTimer+ is a floating overlay app that keeps a countdown timer, tally counter, and stopwatch on top of any app you use. Your timer stays visible while you do something else — drag it anywhere on screen, set size, opacity, and background color per item, or collapse it into a bubble marked with the app icon that reopens wherever you tap it.

Shrink that bubble to the icon alone, or push it to the screen edge and it tucks half out of view until you want it back.

Play, pause, and reset straight from the overlay menu without opening the app, and everything keeps running in the background even when you switch apps or lock the screen. Set it once and keep cooking, exercising, studying, or gaming without ever losing sight of the time.

FAQ

Does the timer stay visible while I use other apps?

Yes. The timer, counter, and stopwatch float on top of any app and run in the background, so they keep going even when you switch apps or lock the screen.

Can I use it as a Pomodoro or workout timer?

Yes. Quick presets include 25 minutes, you can set any duration you like, and you can run several timers at once for work and rest rounds. Pro repeats a timer 2, 3, or 5 times, or endlessly.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited timers, counters, and stopwatches, with no daily time limit. The only free limit is one floating overlay at a time — Pro shows as many as you like at once, or you can watch a short ad for a 24-hour Free Pass.

FloatTimer+

A countdown timer, tally counter, and stopwatch that floats on top of every app.

View on Google Play → Contact us

Overview

FloatTimer+ is a floating overlay app that keeps a countdown timer, tally counter, and stopwatch on top of any app you use. Your timer stays visible while you do something else — drag it anywhere on screen, set size, opacity, and background color per item, or fold it into a compact chip when you need the screen back. Play, pause, and reset straight from the overlay menu without opening the app, and everything keeps running in the background even when you switch apps or lock the screen. Set it once and keep cooking, exercising, studying, or gaming without ever losing sight of the time.

Also available on Windows

FloatTimer+ for Windows Timers, counters and stopwatches that float over any window on Windows as small pastel cards. ›

Key Features

  • Floating overlay — timers, counters, and stopwatches float on any app; drag them anywhere and set size, opacity, and background color per item
  • Multi-timer — any hours/minutes/seconds countdown, with 1, 5, 10, 25, 30 and 60-minute quick presets and as many timers running at once as you like
  • Overlay controls — play, pause, and reset from the overlay menu without opening the app, or fold it into a compact chip and tap to expand
  • Tally counter — tap to count up or down right on the overlay, with a step of 1, 2, 5, or 10 and as many counters as you need
  • Stopwatch — several stopwatches, each timed separately; start, pause, and reset without leaving your current app
  • Alerts and backup — sound, vibration, or a full-screen alert when time is up, plus file export and restore that merges or replaces

FAQ

Does the timer stay visible while I use other apps?

Yes. The timer, counter, and stopwatch float on top of any app and run in the background, so they keep going even when you switch apps or lock the screen.

Can I use it as a Pomodoro or workout timer?

Yes. Quick presets include 25 minutes, you can set any duration you like, and you can run several timers at once for work and rest rounds. Pro repeats a timer 2, 3, or 5 times, or endlessly.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited timers, counters, and stopwatches, with no daily time limit. The only free limit is one floating overlay at a time — Pro shows as many as you like at once, or you can watch a short ad for a 24-hour Free Pass.

Overview

FloatNote+ keeps sticky notes floating on top of any app. Grant the overlay permission once and every note lives as a small chip above your browser, chat, video or home screen; tap it and the chip expands into a full card you can read, edit and share on the spot.

Colors, rich text and inline images keep each note distinct, while the main app works as a library for searching, pinning and reordering. Light and dark themes are both included.

Pair it with FloatNote+ for Windows and notes written on your phone show up on your PC, and notes written on your PC show up on your phone. You just sign in to Google Drive with the same account in both apps, and your notes never pass through a CodeDAC server — they are stored only in your own Google Drive.

FAQ

How do floating notes work?

Grant the overlay permission once and your notes float above other apps as small chips. Tap a chip to expand it into a card and edit it right there; tap outside and it collapses back, saving automatically. Dragging a chip to the zone at the bottom of the screen only stashes the note — real deletion always asks for confirmation.

Where can I use it?

Anywhere on screen — over a browser, a chat, a video, a document or the home screen. You can also create a note without opening the app: from the share sheet, from text you select in another app, or from the Quick Settings tile.

Does it sync with my Windows PC?

Yes. Install FloatNote+ for Windows as well, then in the settings of both apps sign in to Google Drive with the same account and turn on automatic sync. Notes written on your phone then show up on your PC, and notes written on your PC show up on your phone. Your notes never pass through a CodeDAC server — they are stored only in your own Google Drive. Both the note text and the images attached to it travel between devices.

Is it free?

Yes. Saving notes is unlimited and free, and the free version floats one note at a time, gives you all 7 colors and shows ads. Pro adds unlimited floating notes, inline images, adjustable opacity, Google Drive sync, export and import, and removes ads.

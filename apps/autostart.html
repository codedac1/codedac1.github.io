Overview

AutoStart+ is the simplest auto start manager for Android: pick the apps you want and they launch on their own after every reboot, as soon as you unlock the phone — no root, no scripts, no daily tapping.

Search every installed app to add it in seconds, switch each one on or off with a tap, and drag the rows to set the order they start in. You don't have to reboot to test it: the ⋮ menu on any row opens that app right away, and after boot a short message tells you what started, with one tap to run the rest.

Most Android car head units go to sleep instead of restarting when you turn off the car. Turn on Car Mode and your apps open again every time the screen wakes after being off for 3+ minutes.

FAQ

My apps don't launch after boot. What should I do?

Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo and Huawei block background startup by default. AutoStart+ recognises your device and takes you straight to the “Autostart” screen in the phone's own security settings — allow it there and boot launching works. Leaving AutoStart+ out of battery optimisation helps too.

Does AutoStart+ work on Android car head units?

Most Android car head units go to sleep instead of restarting when you turn off the car. Turn on Car Mode and your apps open again every time the screen wakes after being off for 3+ minutes.

Will it drain my battery?

AutoStart+ runs only once at boot and is not an always-on background app, so it has almost no impact on battery life.

Is there an official AutoStart+ APK?

No. AutoStart+ is published only on Google Play, and this site is its official website — we don’t release APK files anywhere else. An APK from another site may be modified or out of date, so please install it from Google Play.

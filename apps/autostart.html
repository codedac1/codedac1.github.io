AutoStart+

Auto start your apps the moment your phone boots. No root, set once, runs forever.

View on Google Play → Contact us

Overview

AutoStart+ is the simplest auto start manager for Android: pick the apps you want and they launch on their own the moment the device finishes booting — no root, no scripts, no daily tapping. Search every installed app to add it in seconds, switch each one on or off with a tap, and drag the rows to set the order they start in. You don't have to reboot to test it: the ⋮ menu on any row opens that app right away, and after boot a short message tells you what started, with one tap to run the rest. The free version auto starts 1 app; PRO lifts the limit so the whole list launches in order, and if you'd rather not subscribe, a short ad unlocks it for 24 hours.

FAQ

My apps don't launch after boot. What should I do?

Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo and Huawei block background startup by default. AutoStart+ recognises your device and takes you straight to the “Autostart” screen in the phone's own security settings — allow it there and boot launching works. Leaving AutoStart+ out of battery optimisation helps too.

How many apps can I auto start?

The free version starts 1 app per boot. PRO removes the limit, so the whole list launches in the order you set. If you'd rather not subscribe, watching a short ad unlocks the limit for 24 hours.

Will it drain my battery?

AutoStart+ runs only once at boot and is not an always-on background app, so it has almost no impact on battery life.

Overview

Clipboard+ is an Android clipboard manager that keeps every text and image you copy in a searchable history, newest first. Since Android 10, no app may read the clipboard in the background, so Clipboard+ saves what you copied the moment it becomes active — when you open the app, tap the floating panel, or use the widget — or right away when you tap Clipboard+ in the text selection menu.

A resizable floating overlay lets you reach your clipboard history from any screen without switching apps. Organize frequently used text into snippets and groups, and paste any clip fast with keyword search, a home screen widget, and a notification panel. You can also export your history to PDF and back up or restore your clips anytime.

Pair it with Clipboard+ for Windows and what you copy on your phone pastes on your PC, and what you copy on your PC pastes on your phone. You just sign in to Google Drive with the same account in both apps, and your clips never pass through a CodeDAC server — they are stored only in your own Google Drive.

FAQ

When does Clipboard+ save what I copy?

Since Android 10, Google blocks apps from reading the clipboard in the background, and no clipboard manager can bypass this. Clipboard+ saves what you copied the moment it becomes active — when you open the app, tap the floating panel, or use the widget. To save without switching apps, select the text and tap Clipboard+ in the selection menu.

Can I access my clipboard history while using other apps?

Yes. A resizable floating overlay lets you reach your history from any screen without switching apps.

Does it sync with my Windows PC?

Yes. Install Clipboard+ for Windows as well, then in the settings of both apps sign in to Google Drive with the same account and turn on automatic sync. From then on, what you copy on your phone pastes on your PC, and what you copy on your PC pastes on your phone. Your clips never pass through a CodeDAC server — they are stored only in your own Google Drive.

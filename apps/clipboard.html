Overview

Clipboard+ is an Android clipboard manager that keeps every text and image you copy in a searchable history, newest first. Since Android 10, no app may read the clipboard in the background, so Clipboard+ saves what you copied the moment it becomes active — when you open the app, tap the floating panel, or use the widget — or right away when you tap Clipboard+ in the text selection menu.

Turn on Auto-save (optional) and Clipboard+ uses Android's Accessibility service to save what you copy in other apps the moment you copy it, without opening the app — it can't read other apps' screens and skips passwords. A resizable floating overlay lets you reach your clipboard history from any screen without switching apps. Organize frequently used text into snippets and groups, and paste any clip fast with keyword search, a home screen widget, and a notification panel.

You can also export your history to PDF and back up or restore your clips anytime. Pair it with Clipboard+ for Windows and what you copy on your phone pastes on your PC, and what you copy on your PC pastes on your phone. You just sign in to Google Drive with the same account in both apps, and your clips never pass through a CodeDAC server — they are stored only in your own Google Drive.

FAQ

When does Clipboard+ save what I copy?

Turn on Auto-save (optional, off by default) and what you copy in other apps is saved the moment you copy it, without opening Clipboard+. It uses Android's Accessibility service only to notice the copy — it can't read other apps' screens, skips clips marked as passwords and sends nothing anywhere. Without it, Android 10+ lets apps read the clipboard only while they're on screen, so Clipboard+ saves when you open the app, tap the floating bubble or use the widget, or when you choose Clipboard+ in the text selection menu.

Can I access my clipboard history while using other apps?

Yes. A resizable floating overlay lets you reach your history from any screen without switching apps.

Does it sync with my Windows PC?

Yes. Install Clipboard+ for Windows as well, then in the settings of both apps sign in to Google Drive with the same account and turn on automatic sync. From then on, what you copy on your phone pastes on your PC, and what you copy on your PC pastes on your phone. Your clips never pass through a CodeDAC server — they are stored only in your own Google Drive.

Overview

FloatCrypto+ for Windows is the desktop version of the Android FloatCrypto+ app. It keeps a slim price ticker on top of every window — your browser, your documents, your video call — showing each coin's symbol and 24-hour change, colored by direction. No tab to switch to, no page to reload.

Drag the ticker anywhere, adjust its opacity and text size, or collapse it into a small bubble that tucks against the edge of the screen; it never takes focus from the app you are typing in. Hover the tray icon for a full table of prices, even with the ticker turned off. Set a target above or below the current price and get a desktop notification the moment the market crosses it, one-time or repeating.

Pick coins from the hundreds of pairs traded on Binance, drag them into your own order, and double-click any coin for a chart with the period high, low, open and change. Prices come from Binance's public market data with automatic fallback to alternate servers and CoinGecko, may be delayed by up to 30 seconds, and are for information only — FloatCrypto+ is not a trading app and nothing in it is investment advice.

FAQ

Is this the same app as FloatCrypto+ on Android?

It is the same app built for Windows, with the same floating ticker, price alerts, watchlist and charts. The two are separate apps, each from its own store, and they do not sync — your watchlist and alerts are stored on the device you set them up on.

Does the ticker get in the way of what I am doing?

No. The ticker is a floating strip that never takes focus, so clicking or dragging it leaves your cursor exactly where you were typing. Lower its opacity, shrink the text, collapse it into a bubble that tucks against the screen edge, or hide it with Ctrl+Alt+T — and the tray icon still shows every price when you hover over it.

Where do the prices come from?

From Binance's public market data, with automatic fallback to alternate servers and CoinGecko so the ticker keeps running when one source is unreachable. Prices may be delayed by up to 30 seconds and are for information only. FloatCrypto+ does not trade, connect to an exchange account or hold any funds, and nothing in it is investment advice.

Overview

FloatCrypto+ floats a live crypto ticker on top of every app, so you can check the market while you watch YouTube, read the news or reply to messages — no switching, no reloading. Drag it anywhere, resize it and dial the opacity down so it never gets in the way.

Set an upper or lower target and it notifies you the moment the market crosses it, keep a watchlist of the coins you actually follow, and open 1-day, 7-day and 30-day charts with period high, low, open and change. Prices come from Binance with an automatic fallback, so the ticker keeps working when one source is unreachable. Market data is delayed by up to 10 seconds and is for reference only, not investment advice.

FAQ

Can I see prices while using other apps?

Yes. The ticker floats on top of every app, so you can watch the market while you are on YouTube, in a game or in a chat — no switching, no reloading. Drag it anywhere, resize it, and lower the opacity so it never covers what you are reading.

How do price alerts work?

Set an upper or lower target and FloatCrypto+ notifies you the moment the market crosses it. You can add an alert straight from a coin card — tap ⋮ and the coin is already filled in — and a bell badge marks every coin you are watching for.

Do I need an account or a wallet?

No. There is no sign-up, no email and no wallet connection.

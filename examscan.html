One invigilator phone keeps watch

ExamScan is an app for invigilators in the exam room. Using Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals it finds AI glasses, wireless earbuds, smartwatches, phones and hotspots, vibrates when a new device appears, and helps you walk to the seat.

WHY NOW

Cheating tools in the exam room have changed

AI glasses with a camera and microphone look like ordinary glasses, and wireless earbuds and smartwatches are too small to spot by eye.

You can't tell them apart by eye

AI glasses look almost the same as regular glasses. Walking around the room only goes so far.

Equipment is costly and heavy to run

Radio detectors cost money per unit and need to be placed and managed in every exam room.

Nothing is recorded

You need a record of when, where and how a suspicious situation was checked, to explain it later.

HOW IT WORKS

Three steps with one invigilator phone

  1. 1

    Pre-exam check

    Before the exam, take one look around the room to find suspicious devices early. Devices in the hallway or next room and classroom equipment are set aside.

  2. 2

    Quiet monitoring

    During the exam the screen just shows “All clear”. When a new wireless device appears, the phone vibrates and shows its type and distance (near, medium, far).

  3. 3

    Find it, keep a record

    Follow the signal bars and vibration to the seat. Where it was found and what was done is kept as a record that can't be edited, and can be exported as a PDF report.

WHAT IT WATCHES

What it can watch

ExamScan gives you a signal that needs checking, not a verdict. The invigilator makes the final call.

  • AI glasses (smart glasses with Bluetooth on, such as Meta Ray-Ban)
  • Wireless earbuds (waiting to connect or advertising)
  • Smartwatches
  • Phones with Bluetooth on
  • Phone hotspots and Wi-Fi cameras

RECORDS & PRIVACY

Keeps a record, not personal data

Exam records that can't be edited

The original is never changed; later judgments are only appended as dated review notes.

PDF report and replay

Export the timeline, where devices were found and what was done as a PDF, and replay how the exam went.

Records stay on this phone

Nothing is sent to a server. No ad or analytics SDKs, and you choose how long records are kept.

No student names

Only seat or exam numbers are written down. Sharing is done by the invigilator under school rules.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need any extra equipment?

No. The invigilator's Android phone is enough. It only uses Bluetooth and location permission (Android requires it to read signal strength).

Does it collect students' personal data?

No. Records are stored only on the invigilator's phone and are never sent to our servers. Seat or exam numbers are used instead of student names.

Does it catch everything?

No. Devices that send almost no signal, such as locked phones or cheap earbuds that are already connected, are hard to catch. We state these limits up front, in the app and on this page. ExamScan helps invigilation; it is not a judging device.
